# Checking the testi unici correspondences: registro / versamenti (and tributi erariali minori)

Research date: 22-10-2026. Sources: **Normattiva** (texts "vigente al 22-10-2026", obtained through `esporta/attoCompleto` and checked on article deep links) and **Gazzetta Ufficiale** (original text, used for the bollo tariff in Allegato 3 to D.Lgs. 123/2025, which Normattiva does not show as text, and for checks against the original text of TU 123 artt. 88, 116 and 205). No secondary sources were used.

URL conventions:
- New provisions: `https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:2025-08-01;123:1~artNN` (`:1` = the allegato/testo unico; the same pattern applies to `2025-03-24;33:1` and `2024-11-05;174:1`).
- Old provisions: the `!vig=2026-10-22` suffix forces today's in-force text. Without it, Normattiva's article deep link opens the future version, which reads «ARTICOLO ABROGATO DAL D.LGS. … COME MODIFICATO DAL D.L. 31 DICEMBRE 2025, N. 200».
- Commi of L. 232/2016 and L. 228/2012: the deep link opens art. 1, but the comma is loaded lazily. The text was checked in the full export (URL in the notes).
